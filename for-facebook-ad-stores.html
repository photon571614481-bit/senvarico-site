A Store Built for Facebook Ad-Driven Selling

Stores that live on Facebook and Instagram ads need low fixed costs, clean product pages and international checkout. How Senvarico fits: ¥0 or ¥99/month, 0% platform fee, PayPal and Stripe, multi-currency.

A Facebook ad-driven store is a landing page with a checkout. Traffic is bought, not earned, so every fixed cost and every percentage taken from a sale changes whether a campaign is profitable. The store needs to be reliable, quick to edit and priced for a small catalog.

This is probably you if

  • Most of your traffic comes from Facebook and Instagram ads
  • You sell 10–100 SKUs, often in one or two niches
  • Your customers are in Europe and North America
  • You want to keep the platform cost out of your margin calculation

How Senvarico fits

Fixed cost, not a percentage

Senvarico costs ¥0/month (20 SKUs) or ¥99/month (100 SKUs) with a 0% platform transaction fee. Your margin per order is ad cost plus payment processing, not a platform commission on top.

Product pages that load without JavaScript bloat

Storefronts are lightweight, which matters when a large share of visitors arrive from mobile ad clicks.

International checkout

PayPal and Stripe, multi-currency display and multi-language pages cover the markets most Facebook campaigns target.

Chinese or English admin

Run the store in whichever language the team works in.

When it is not the right fit

  • You rely on a deep app stack for upsells, bundles and post-purchase flows
  • You need thousands of SKUs or a multi-brand setup
  • You need built-in advanced attribution tooling

For a large app ecosystem around Facebook ads, Shopify is the broadest option; for a Chinese-language full platform, Shoplazza or SHOPLINE.

How to do it

  1. Set up on the Free plan, connect PayPal and Stripe, set currencies for your target markets.
  2. Build one focused product page per campaign; keep shipping and returns visible.
  3. Run campaigns; track profit as ad cost plus gateway fee, since there is no platform fee.
  4. Move to the Small Seller plan when the winning catalog passes 20 SKUs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Senvarico suitable for Facebook ad-driven stores?

Yes. Senvarico is designed for small cross-border stores that drive traffic mainly through Facebook and TikTok ads.

How much does Senvarico cost?

Senvarico has two plans: a Free plan at ¥0/month with up to 20 SKUs, and a Small Seller plan at ¥99/month with up to 100 SKUs. Both have a 0% platform transaction fee. There is no separate annual price at this time.

Does Senvarico charge a platform transaction fee?

No. Senvarico charges a 0% platform transaction fee on both plans. Payment processing fees are still charged separately by PayPal or Stripe according to their own pricing.

Does Senvarico support multiple currencies?

Yes. Senvarico storefronts support multiple currencies.
