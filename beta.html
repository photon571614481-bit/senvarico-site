Get Started with Senvarico (Beta)

Senvarico is open for use in beta. Open a store by email: Free plan ¥0/month (Up to 20 SKUs), Small Seller plan ¥99/month (Up to 100 SKUs), 0% platform transaction fee.

How to open a store

  1. Use the button below. The email template asks for your store name, product category, SKU count, target markets, plan and payment methods.
  2. We reply with your access details and admin login.
  3. Log in, add products, connect PayPal or Stripe, set currencies and shipping regions, and start selling.

Which plan

  • Free: ¥0/month, Up to 20 SKUs, 0% platform transaction fee. Sellers who are just starting to test products.
  • Small Seller: ¥99/month, Up to 100 SKUs, 0% platform transaction fee, multi-language and multi-currency. Small teams that have started operating steadily.

Payment processing fees are charged separately by payment providers such as PayPal and Stripe. Senvarico does not add an additional platform transaction fee. No separate free trial, because a permanent free plan already exists.

What beta means

Senvarico can be used for real sales, but the product is still being refined: the feature scope is what the features page lists, and it improves with feedback. If your store needs thousands of SKUs or enterprise workflows, Senvarico is not the right fit.

Share only what the enquiry needs. Do not send passwords, payment card details or customer personal data by email.
