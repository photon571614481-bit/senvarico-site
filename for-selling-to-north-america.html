A Lightweight Store for Small Sellers Targeting North America

Selling to US and Canadian customers as a small cross-border seller: USD and CAD display, PayPal and Stripe, fast pages for ad traffic, low fixed cost. How Senvarico fits and where it does not.

North America is the most common first market for cross-border sellers testing products through TikTok and Facebook ads. Buyers expect prices in their currency, familiar checkout and clear shipping expectations. The store should deliver that without a large platform bill while the seller is still finding product-market fit.

This is probably you if

  • You sell to the United States and Canada
  • You test or run 10–100 SKUs
  • Most traffic comes from paid social
  • You want a Chinese or English admin and no server to manage

How Senvarico fits

USD and CAD, PayPal and Stripe

Multi-currency display and the two checkout options North American buyers know best, with no platform commission on top of their fees.

Lightweight pages for ad clicks

Storefronts are lightweight, which helps mobile visitors arriving from ads.

Free while testing

The Free plan (20 SKUs) costs ¥0/month; the Small Seller plan (100 SKUs) is ¥99/month.

Run it from anywhere

Chinese and English admin; SaaS hosted.

When it is not the right fit

  • You need automated US sales-tax calculation built into the platform
  • You need Amazon or Walmart marketplace integrations
  • You need thousands of SKUs

For built-in tax automation and marketplace integrations, Shopify is the broader option; Square Online suits sellers who already use Square in person in the US.

How to do it

  1. Set USD (and CAD if relevant) as storefront currencies and connect PayPal and Stripe.
  2. List test products with clear shipping times to the US and Canada.
  3. Run TikTok or Facebook campaigns to product pages.
  4. Upgrade when the winning catalog passes 20 SKUs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Senvarico designed for cross-border ecommerce?

Yes. Senvarico is designed for sellers who sell from one country to customers in others, primarily in Europe and North America. Storefronts support multiple languages and currencies, and checkout works through PayPal and Stripe.

Does Senvarico support multiple currencies?

Yes. Senvarico storefronts support multiple currencies.

Does Senvarico support Stripe?

Yes. Senvarico supports Stripe for checkout. Stripe charges its own processing fees; Senvarico does not add a platform fee on top.

Is Senvarico suitable for TikTok product testing?

Yes. Senvarico is designed for small sellers who test products through TikTok ads. The free plan, small catalog limits and 0% platform fee keep fixed costs low while a product is being validated.
