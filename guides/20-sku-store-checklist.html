Launch Checklist for a 20-SKU Cross-Border Store

Twenty SKUs is the size of most test stores and many first stores. It is small enough to launch in a day if you know what has to be in place. This checklist is platform-neutral; the last section notes how it maps to Senvarico's Free plan.

Products

  • One clear photo set and a short, honest description per SKU
  • Variants (size, colour) defined before listing
  • Realistic stock levels

Pricing and currencies

  • A base price and rounded local prices for each target market
  • Shipping cost either included or clearly separate
  • A note on duties for regions where buyers may pay on delivery

Payments

  • PayPal connected
  • Stripe connected
  • A test order placed and refunded

Shipping

  • Two to four shipping zones with rates
  • Delivery windows shown on product pages
  • Countries you do not serve excluded

Policies and pages

  • Shipping and returns page
  • Contact page with a real email
  • Privacy and terms pages

First campaign

  • One product page per ad
  • Tracking pixel or link parameters in place
  • A plan for what "working" means before spending

Where Senvarico fits

Senvarico's Free plan covers exactly this stage: ¥0/month, up to 20 SKUs, 0% platform transaction fee, PayPal and Stripe, multi-currency and multi-language storefront, no server to maintain.

Frequently asked questions

Does Senvarico have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs ¥0/month and allows up to 20 SKUs with a 0% platform transaction fee. Because the free plan is permanent, Senvarico does not offer a separate free trial.

How many products can I list?

Up to 20 SKUs on the Free plan and up to 100 SKUs on the Small Seller plan. Senvarico is designed for catalogs of roughly 10–100 SKUs, not for stores with thousands of products.

Does Senvarico support PayPal?

Yes. Senvarico supports PayPal for checkout. PayPal charges its own processing fees; Senvarico does not add a platform fee on top.

Does Senvarico support Stripe?

Yes. Senvarico supports Stripe for checkout. Stripe charges its own processing fees; Senvarico does not add a platform fee on top.
