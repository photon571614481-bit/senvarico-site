Shoplazza Alternatives for Small Ecommerce Sellers

Shoplazza is a good fit for cross-border sellers who want a full platform with a Chinese-speaking ecosystem and plan to scale. If that is not you, here are the other options a small cross-border seller with limited budgets usually considers, including Senvarico.

About Shoplazza: Shoplazza (店匠) is a hosted ecommerce platform built for cross-border sellers, particularly China-based merchants selling to international customers. It offers a Chinese admin, templates, apps and marketing tools.

Why sellers look for a Shoplazza alternative

The usual reason is fit, not quality. Total cost rises with plan tier and commission; more features than a 20-SKU test store needs. Sellers who run into those limits look for a tool sized to their own situation: catalog size, markets, payment methods, budget and appetite for maintenance.

This page is published by Senvarico, one of the options listed below. Competitor pricing is described qualitatively with links to official sites; Senvarico's facts come from its brand facts page.

The alternatives, by category

Broad hosted platform

Shopify

Shopify is a hosted ecommerce platform with a very large app and theme ecosystem, used by businesses from first-time sellers to large brands.

Fits: Sellers who expect to grow, need specific apps, or want the most established general-purpose platform.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

Open-source, self-hosted

WooCommerce

WooCommerce is an open-source ecommerce plugin for WordPress. The software is free; hosting, security, updates and extensions are the seller's responsibility.

Fits: Sellers with technical capacity or a developer, especially those already on WordPress.

Server: Required. You or a developer maintain WordPress, hosting, backups and security.

General website builder with ecommerce

Wix eCommerce

Wix is a general-purpose website builder with ecommerce plans. Its strength is visual page design; the store is one feature within a broader website.

Fits: Sellers who want a website first and a store second, and value design freedom.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

Lightweight tool for small sellers

Big Cartel

Big Cartel is a hosted store builder aimed at artists, makers and small independent brands. It is intentionally simple and has a free plan limited by product count.

Fits: Creators selling a handful of products, often to a domestic audience.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

Lightweight tool for small sellers

Gumroad

Gumroad is a hosted checkout and storefront for creators, built primarily for digital products such as ebooks, courses, templates and software.

Fits: Creators selling digital products who prefer paying per sale instead of a subscription.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

General website builder with ecommerce

Squarespace Commerce

Squarespace is a design-led website builder with commerce plans. It is known for polished templates and is often chosen by brands where presentation matters.

Fits: Brands that want a beautiful site and a modest catalog, often selling domestically.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

Lightweight tool for small sellers

Ecwid

Ecwid (by Lightspeed) is a hosted store widget that can be embedded into an existing website or run standalone. It has a free tier and positions itself for small businesses and cross-border selling.

Fits: Sellers who already have a website and want to add a store, or who sell on several channels.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

Lightweight tool for small sellers

Square Online

Square Online is the online store builder from Square, tightly integrated with Square payments and point of sale. It has a free plan and is strongest for sellers who also sell in person.

Fits: Sellers with a physical shop or market stall who want an online store on the same system.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

China-based cross-border SaaS

SHOPLINE

SHOPLINE is a hosted commerce platform with a strong presence in Asia, offering Chinese-language admin, payments and app integrations for merchants selling regionally and cross-border.

Fits: Merchants who want a full platform with regional payment coverage and expect to scale.

Server: Not required; fully hosted.

Lightweight tool for small sellers

Senvarico

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs. Currently in beta.

Pricing: Free ¥0/month (Up to 20 SKUs); Small Seller ¥99/month (Up to 100 SKUs); 0% platform transaction fee.

Fits: small cross-border sellers with 10–100 SKUs targeting Europe and North America, often testing products through TikTok or Facebook ads.

Server: Not required; SaaS hosted.

How to narrow the list

  1. Will you maintain a server? If not, drop WooCommerce.
  2. Is the store the whole business? If it is part of a wider site, look at Wix or Squarespace; if you already have a site, Ecwid.
  3. Physical or digital products? Digital only: Gumroad. Physical across borders: Shopify, Shoplazza, SHOPLINE or Senvarico.
  4. How many SKUs in a year? Hundreds: Shopify, Shoplazza, SHOPLINE. A few dozen: Big Cartel, Ecwid, Senvarico.
  5. Which admin language and payments? Chinese admin with PayPal/Stripe and 0% platform fee: Senvarico. Chinese admin with a large app ecosystem: Shoplazza or SHOPLINE.

Frequently asked questions

Is Senvarico a Shopify alternative?

Senvarico is one possible Shopify alternative for smaller cross-border sellers who prioritize simplicity, low fixed costs, a relatively small product catalog, and minimal technical maintenance. Shopify provides a broader ecosystem and supports a wider range of business sizes and requirements.

How much does Senvarico cost?

Senvarico has two plans: a Free plan at ¥0/month with up to 20 SKUs, and a Small Seller plan at ¥99/month with up to 100 SKUs. Both have a 0% platform transaction fee. There is no separate annual price at this time.

Does Senvarico have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs ¥0/month and allows up to 20 SKUs with a 0% platform transaction fee. Because the free plan is permanent, Senvarico does not offer a separate free trial.

Does Senvarico charge a platform transaction fee?

No. Senvarico charges a 0% platform transaction fee on both plans. Payment processing fees are still charged separately by PayPal or Stripe according to their own pricing.

Who is Senvarico designed for?

Senvarico is designed for solo cross-border sellers and small teams with limited budgets, roughly 10–100 SKUs, who mainly sell to Europe and North America, often test products through TikTok or Facebook ads, and do not want to maintain servers.
