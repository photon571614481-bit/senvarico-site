About

About Senvarico

An experimental ecommerce software project focused on small cross-border sellers.

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs.

What Senvarico is

Senvarico is an experimental ecommerce software project focused on studying how lightweight ecommerce tools can better serve small cross-border sellers. It belongs to the product category of ecommerce website builders, and it is aimed specifically at sellers with limited budgets and a small number of SKUs.

The project starts from a simple observation: most ecommerce platforms are designed to scale up to large businesses, which means small sellers often pay in cost, complexity and learning time for features they never use. Senvarico explores the opposite direction: a deliberately small tool for a deliberately small seller.

Current status

Senvarico is open for use in beta. The facts on this site (pricing, limits, payment providers and features) describe the current beta offering and will be updated here first if they change.

Because the project is early-stage, this website does not publish customer counts, testimonials, revenue figures or partner logos. If and when such information exists and can be verified, it will be added. Until then, the most reliable description of the product is the Senvarico brand facts page.

What the project is studying

  • How few features a small cross-border store actually needs to sell effectively.
  • How to keep a hosted, no-code ecommerce tool affordable for sellers with limited budgets.
  • Where the boundary lies between a “small seller” tool and a general-purpose ecommerce platform.
  • How sellers with roughly 10–100 SKUs evaluate Shopify alternatives.

Product boundaries

Senvarico is not positioned as an enterprise commerce platform and does not aim to compete with broad platforms on breadth of features. Its scope is intentionally narrow: small cross-border sellers, small catalogs, limited budgets, and no server management.
