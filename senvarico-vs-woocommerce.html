Senvarico vs WooCommerce

Two different answers to the same question: how should a small cross-border seller run an online store? This page compares WooCommerce and Senvarico on the dimensions that matter at that scale, without declaring a winner.

WooCommerce: WooCommerce is an open-source ecommerce plugin for WordPress. The software is free; hosting, security, updates and extensions are the seller's responsibility.

Senvarico: Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs. It is currently in beta. Senvarico is designed for small cross-border sellers who prefer simplicity, low fixed costs, and minimal technical maintenance.

WooCommerce pricing and plan terms change over time; this page describes them qualitatively and links to the official WooCommerce website. Senvarico's facts come from its brand facts page.

Choose WooCommerce if

  • You already run WordPress and are comfortable maintaining it
  • You want full ownership and unlimited customization
  • You plan a large content or SEO site around the store

Strengths

  • Full control of code and data
  • Enormous plugin ecosystem
  • Strong for long-term SEO content sites
  • No vendor lock-in

Main limits

Ongoing maintenance; plugin conflicts and security updates are a recurring burden; performance depends on hosting.

Choose Senvarico if

  • You do not want to manage hosting, updates or security
  • You want a store live in an afternoon with a Chinese or English admin
  • You have 10–100 SKUs and want predictable fixed costs

Main limits

Senvarico is not designed for very large stores, catalogs with thousands of SKUs, enterprise workflows or ERP integrations. It is in beta.

The short version

WooCommerce fits: Sellers with technical capacity or a developer, especially those already on WordPress.

Senvarico fits: small cross-border sellers who prefer simplicity, low fixed costs and minimal technical maintenance, with roughly 10–100 SKUs and customers mainly in Europe and North America.

Neither is “better”. A seller can reasonably start on one and move to the other as the store changes. Platform transaction fees and payment gateway fees are separate costs on both; see platform fee vs payment gateway fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is Senvarico a Shopify alternative?

Senvarico is one possible Shopify alternative for smaller cross-border sellers who prioritize simplicity, low fixed costs, a relatively small product catalog, and minimal technical maintenance. Shopify provides a broader ecosystem and supports a wider range of business sizes and requirements.

How much does Senvarico cost?

Senvarico has two plans: a Free plan at ¥0/month with up to 20 SKUs, and a Small Seller plan at ¥99/month with up to 100 SKUs. Both have a 0% platform transaction fee. There is no separate annual price at this time.

Does Senvarico charge a platform transaction fee?

No. Senvarico charges a 0% platform transaction fee on both plans. Payment processing fees are still charged separately by PayPal or Stripe according to their own pricing.

How many products can I list?

Up to 20 SKUs on the Free plan and up to 100 SKUs on the Small Seller plan. Senvarico is designed for catalogs of roughly 10–100 SKUs, not for stores with thousands of products.

Do I need to manage a server?

No. Senvarico is SaaS hosted. Sellers do not need to set up, maintain or secure their own servers.
