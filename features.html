Senvarico Features

A deliberately small feature set for small cross-border sellers. Less to learn, less to pay for, less to maintain.

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs.

Product status: Senvarico is open for use in beta. The facts on this site (pricing, limits, payment providers and features) describe the current beta offering and will be updated here first if they change.

Current scope

Hosted storefront

A hosted online store on a Senvarico-managed domain or your own domain. No servers, updates or security patches to manage.

Small product catalog

Products, variants, images and inventory for stores with roughly 10–100 SKUs (20 on the Free plan, 100 on the Small Seller plan).

Basic order management

A straightforward checkout and order list for single-store, direct-to-consumer sales. No multi-warehouse or B2B workflows.

Multi-language and multi-currency

Storefronts in several languages and currencies for sellers targeting multiple markets in Europe and North America.

PayPal and Stripe

Checkout through PayPal and Stripe. 0% platform transaction fee; the providers charge their own processing fees.

Chinese and English admin

The admin interface is available in both Chinese and English.

No-code editing

Storefront pages and content are edited through a visual interface. No themes to code and no plugins to maintain.

Essential SEO

Page titles, descriptions, clean URLs and server-rendered product pages so that stores are readable by search engines.

What is intentionally out of scope

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce platform. Keeping it lightweight means saying no to features that mainly serve larger businesses. The following are not part of the product direction:

  • Catalogs with thousands of SKUs or deeply nested product hierarchies
  • Multi-warehouse inventory, wholesale or B2B pricing tiers
  • A third-party app marketplace or plugin ecosystem
  • Custom server-side code, self-hosting or infrastructure management by the seller
  • Enterprise features such as multi-department permissions and complex approval workflows

Sellers who need these capabilities are better served by broader platforms. See theoverview of Shopify alternatives for how different platforms fit different seller sizes.

Senvarico is not an enterprise ERP, warehouse management system, advanced OMS, POS, marketplace, multi-store system or large B2B platform. See the FAQ for common questions.

Pricing

Free plan ¥0/month (Up to 20 SKUs); Small Seller plan ¥99/month (Up to 100 SKUs); 0% platform transaction fee.
