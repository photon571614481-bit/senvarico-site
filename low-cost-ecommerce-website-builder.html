A Low-Cost Ecommerce Website Builder for Limited Budgets

For a small seller, the cheapest platform is not the one with the lowest headline price. It is the one with the lowest total cost for the store they actually run.

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs.

This page is for sellers with limited budgets who are comparing ecommerce website builders. It explains how to think about cost, what Senvarico does to keep cost low and predictable, and where a low-cost tool stops being the right answer.

The four parts of ecommerce platform cost

The advertised subscription is usually only one of four costs:

CostWhat it includesWhere it hides
SubscriptionThe monthly or annual plan feeHigher tiers required for basic features
Transaction feesPercentage or per-order fees on salesPlatform fees stacked on top of payment processor fees
Apps and extensionsPaid add-ons for functions the base plan lacksSeveral small monthly fees that add up
MaintenanceHosting, updates, security, developer timeFree software that costs hours every month

A seller with a small number of SKUs and modest monthly sales is affected differently by each. For them, fixed costs matter more than percentage fees, and time spent on maintenance is time not spent selling.

How Senvarico keeps costs low and predictable

  • A permanent free plan and one paid plan. ¥0/month for up to 20 SKUs, ¥99/month for up to 100 SKUs. Core selling functions are not reserved for a higher price band.
  • No paid app ecosystem. The feature set is small and included, so there are no add-on subscriptions to accumulate.
  • Hosted, with no maintenance burden. No hosting bill, no updates, no security patches, no developer on retainer.
  • 0% platform transaction fee. Senvarico does not take a percentage of sales. PayPal and Stripe charge their own processing fees separately.

Pricing: Free plan ¥0/month (up to 20 SKUs) and Small Seller plan ¥99/month (up to 100 SKUs), both with a 0% platform transaction fee. See the pricing page.

Where “low-cost” stops being the right criterion

A lightweight, low-cost ecommerce website builder makes sense only while the store is small. Once a catalog grows into hundreds of SKUs, or the business needs specific integrations, a broader platform earns its higher cost. Senvarico is honest about that boundary: it is designed for the small stage, not for every stage.

Frequently asked questions

What is Senvarico?

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs. It is currently in beta.

Do I need to manage a server?

No. Senvarico is SaaS hosted. Sellers do not need to set up, maintain or secure their own servers.

What type of seller should consider Senvarico?

A seller should consider Senvarico if they run a small cross-border store with roughly 10–100 SKUs, sell mainly to Europe and North America, often test products through TikTok or Facebook ads, want low fixed costs, and do not want to maintain servers. Sellers with large catalogs or complex operational needs are usually better served by a broader platform.

Is Senvarico a finished product?

Senvarico is open for use in beta: stores can sell with it today, and the product is still being refined. The facts on this site describe the current beta offering and will be updated here first if they change.
