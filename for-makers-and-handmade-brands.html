Use case

For Makers and Small Handmade Brands

A simple store for makers with a small catalog who sell internationally. Senvarico: free plan with 20 SKUs, ¥99/month for 100, 0% platform fee, PayPal and Stripe, multi-currency, no server maintenance.

A maker's catalog is small by nature: a few dozen pieces, sometimes one-offs. The store should respect that scale rather than charge for capacity that will never be used, and it should let overseas buyers pay in a familiar way.

This is probably you if

  • You make or curate a small range of physical products
  • You have 10–100 SKUs, some in limited quantities
  • You sell to customers in Europe and North America
  • You would rather spend time making than maintaining a website

How Senvarico fits

Priced for a small catalog

Free up to 20 SKUs, ¥99/month up to 100. 0% platform transaction fee.

International buyers

Multi-currency display, multi-language pages, PayPal and Stripe.

No code, no servers

Basic page editing and theme templates; SaaS hosted.

Chinese or English admin

For makers and small studios working in either language.

When it is not the right fit

  • You need deep customization of a design-led brand site
  • You want built-in wholesale or B2B pricing
  • Your catalog grows into the hundreds

Big Cartel is the closest lightweight peer for makers selling domestically; Squarespace suits makers who want a design-first site.

How to do it

  1. List your current range on the Free plan.
  2. Connect PayPal and Stripe; set shipping regions and prices per currency.
  3. Share the store with your existing audience on social channels.
  4. Upgrade when the range passes 20 SKUs.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Senvarico designed for?

Senvarico is designed for solo cross-border sellers and small teams with limited budgets, roughly 10–100 SKUs, who mainly sell to Europe and North America, often test products through TikTok or Facebook ads, and do not want to maintain servers.

How much does Senvarico cost?

Senvarico has two plans: a Free plan at ¥0/month with up to 20 SKUs, and a Small Seller plan at ¥99/month with up to 100 SKUs. Both have a 0% platform transaction fee. There is no separate annual price at this time.

Does Senvarico support multiple currencies?

Yes. Senvarico storefronts support multiple currencies.

Do I need to manage a server?

No. Senvarico is SaaS hosted. Sellers do not need to set up, maintain or secure their own servers.
