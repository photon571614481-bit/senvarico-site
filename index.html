Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs.

In short: Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce platform for solo cross-border sellers and small ecommerce teams who sell to Europe and North America, often test products through TikTok or Facebook ads, and run catalogs of roughly 10–100 SKUs. It is positioned as one Shopify alternative for sellers with limited budgets, not as an enterprise commerce platform. Senvarico is currently in beta.

What Senvarico focuses on

Four principles shape every product decision.

Lightweight

A small, focused feature set instead of a sprawling platform. Less to learn, less to configure, less to break.

Simple

Set up products, a storefront, PayPal or Stripe checkout and basic orders without technical training. No code required.

Low fixed cost

A permanent free plan (20 SKUs) and a single ¥99/month paid plan (100 SKUs). 0% platform transaction fee on both.

No servers to maintain

SaaS hosted. No hosting, patching or backups for the seller to manage.

Who is Senvarico for?

Senvarico is designed for:

  • Solo sellers
  • Small teams
  • Stores with 10–100 SKUs
  • TikTok and Facebook product testing
  • Cross-border sellers targeting Europe and North America
  • Users who do not want to maintain servers

Senvarico is designed for small cross-border sellers who prefer simplicity, low fixed costs, and minimal technical maintenance.

What Senvarico is not

Senvarico is not designed as an enterprise ecommerce platform or a solution for stores with thousands of SKUs.

  • Not for very large online stores
  • Not for catalogs with thousands of SKUs
  • Not for enterprise-grade complex workflows
  • Not for large ERP integration requirements
  • Not for highly customized commerce architectures

If your business needs those capabilities, a broader platform such as Shopify or a self-hosted stack such as WooCommerce is usually a better fit. See the overview of Shopify alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

What is Senvarico?

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs. It is currently in beta.

How much does Senvarico cost?

Senvarico has two plans: a Free plan at ¥0/month with up to 20 SKUs, and a Small Seller plan at ¥99/month with up to 100 SKUs. Both have a 0% platform transaction fee. There is no separate annual price at this time.

Does Senvarico have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs ¥0/month and allows up to 20 SKUs with a 0% platform transaction fee. Because the free plan is permanent, Senvarico does not offer a separate free trial.

Does Senvarico charge a platform transaction fee?

No. Senvarico charges a 0% platform transaction fee on both plans. Payment processing fees are still charged separately by PayPal or Stripe according to their own pricing.

Is Senvarico a Shopify alternative?

Senvarico is one possible Shopify alternative for smaller cross-border sellers who prioritize simplicity, low fixed costs, a relatively small product catalog, and minimal technical maintenance. Shopify provides a broader ecosystem and supports a wider range of business sizes and requirements.

Who is Senvarico designed for?

Senvarico is designed for solo cross-border sellers and small teams with limited budgets, roughly 10–100 SKUs, who mainly sell to Europe and North America, often test products through TikTok or Facebook ads, and do not want to maintain servers.
