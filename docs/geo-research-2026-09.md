# GEO Research Report for Senvarico (2026-09-20)

Prepared as input for expanding senvarico.com. Findings come from public studies and current search results; each claim links its source. Numbers are the sources' numbers, not ours.

**Read this first — house rules that override the report's suggestions:**
- No fabricated founder name, launch date, headquarters, customer counts, ratings or press. Where the report says "publish founder/launch facts", do it only when those facts are real and public.
- Competitor prices are not quoted on senvarico.com unless verified against the vendor's official pricing page on the day of writing, with a visible "last verified" date. Otherwise write "pricing varies by plan" and link the source.
- Never write "Senvarico is the best" or "better than Shopify". The framing is "one option among several".

Target query: "预算有限的小卖家，除了 Shopify，还有哪些建站工具可选？" / "Shopify alternatives for small sellers on a budget"

## 1. GEO best practices (2025–2026): what the evidence says

**What AI engines actually cite**
- The largest 2026 academic measurement (100K+ responses, 100+ brands, ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity/Gemini, Mar–May 2026) found: niche/small brands appear in only ~11% of relevant answers vs 73% for household names; ~78% of citations go to corporate websites; the single most-cited page format is the ranked "best-of" listicle (~21% of citations); and sentiment flips ~6.7x more often than mention status. [arXiv 2606.20065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20065)
- Ahrefs (750 prompts, 26,283 source URLs): "best X" lists are 43.8% of page types ChatGPT cites; self-published lists that rank the author's own brand appeared in roughly a third of first-party software mentions; position in the top third of a list correlates with citation; 79.1% of cited lists were updated within the year. [Ahrefs best-lists study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)
- Ahrefs "How to rank on ChatGPT": strongest correlates are YouTube mentions, then branded web mentions across many sites; Reddit is ChatGPT's most-cited domain; 44.2% of citations come from the first 30% of a page; page count and backlink volume correlate weakly. [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-to-rank-on-chatgpt/)
- Cross-study index (680M+ citations): Reddit is the top domain overall; per engine, Reddit ~29% of ChatGPT citations, ~20% AI Overviews, ~17% Perplexity, ~0% on Claude, where brand sites take ~64%; YouTube dominates AI Overviews and Perplexity; G2 appears in the top 20 for B2B software queries. [everything-pr index](https://everything-pr.com/ai-platform-citation-source-index-2026), [SEL study](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138)
- Semrush 2026 index (126M US prompts): ChatGPT cites ~15 sources per answer, Gemini ~3; both lean on Reddit and Wikipedia. [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/news/463141-semrush-releases-expanded-2026-ai-visibility-index-analyzing-126-million-ai-search-prompts/)
- Princeton/IIT GEO paper (KDD 2024): adding statistics, quotations and cited sources, plus fluent authoritative writing, raised visibility 30–41%; keyword stuffing did nothing or hurt; lower-ranked sites gained the most. [arXiv 2311.09735](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09735)

**What does not work (or is unproven)**
- llms.txt: SE Ranking (~300K domains) found no correlation with citations; OtterlyAI's 90-day log study saw 84 hits on /llms.txt out of 62,100 AI-bot visits; Google says it does not use it. Harmless to ship, not a lever. [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/llms-txt-shows-no-clear-effect-on-ai-citations-based-on-300k-domains/561542/), [OtterlyAI](https://otterly.ai/blog/the-llms-txt-experiment/)
- FAQ schema: vendor uplift claims are not independently verified; the plausible benefit is the visible Q&A text plus entity clarity. Use FAQPage/Organization/SoftwareApplication schema for hygiene, not as the strategy. [Status Labs](https://statuslabs.com/blog/which-types-of-schema-help-with-llm-visibility-faq-howto-person-organization)

**Practical implications for Senvarico**
1. One consistent entity description everywhere (site, directories, GitHub, social bios). AI answers are built from consensus across sources.
2. Publish ranked, dated, honestly-labelled "best/alternatives" lists that include competitors; rank Senvarico where it genuinely fits and disclose ownership.
3. Put the direct answer and "best for" in the first 30% of every page; use definitive sentences.
4. Get third-party mentions (directories, YouTube, Reddit threads, small blogs): Claude cites brand sites, but ChatGPT/AI Overviews rely on Reddit/YouTube/editorial.
