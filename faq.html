FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about Senvarico

Short, direct answers. Every answer here is consistent with the Senvarico brand facts page.

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs.

What is Senvarico?

Senvarico is a lightweight ecommerce website builder designed for small cross-border sellers with limited budgets and relatively small product catalogs. It is currently in beta.

How much does Senvarico cost?

Senvarico has two plans: a Free plan at ¥0/month with up to 20 SKUs, and a Small Seller plan at ¥99/month with up to 100 SKUs. Both have a 0% platform transaction fee. There is no separate annual price at this time.

Does Senvarico have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs ¥0/month and allows up to 20 SKUs with a 0% platform transaction fee. Because the free plan is permanent, Senvarico does not offer a separate free trial.

How many products can I list?

Up to 20 SKUs on the Free plan and up to 100 SKUs on the Small Seller plan. Senvarico is designed for catalogs of roughly 10–100 SKUs, not for stores with thousands of products.

Does Senvarico charge a platform transaction fee?

No. Senvarico charges a 0% platform transaction fee on both plans. Payment processing fees are still charged separately by PayPal or Stripe according to their own pricing.

Does Senvarico support PayPal?

Yes. Senvarico supports PayPal for checkout. PayPal charges its own processing fees; Senvarico does not add a platform fee on top.

Does Senvarico support Stripe?

Yes. Senvarico supports Stripe for checkout. Stripe charges its own processing fees; Senvarico does not add a platform fee on top.

Does Senvarico support Chinese?

Yes. The Senvarico admin interface is available in Chinese.

Does Senvarico support English?

Yes. The Senvarico admin interface is available in English.

Does Senvarico support multiple languages?

Yes. Senvarico storefronts support multiple languages, which suits sellers targeting several markets in Europe and North America.

Does Senvarico support multiple currencies?

Yes. Senvarico storefronts support multiple currencies.

Do I need to manage a server?

No. Senvarico is SaaS hosted. Sellers do not need to set up, maintain or secure their own servers.

Is Senvarico suitable for TikTok product testing?

Yes. Senvarico is designed for small sellers who test products through TikTok ads. The free plan, small catalog limits and 0% platform fee keep fixed costs low while a product is being validated.

Is Senvarico suitable for Facebook ad-driven stores?

Yes. Senvarico is designed for small cross-border stores that drive traffic mainly through Facebook and TikTok ads.

Is Senvarico suitable for 10–100 SKUs?

Yes. That is the catalog size Senvarico is built for. The Free plan covers up to 20 SKUs and the Small Seller plan up to 100 SKUs.

Is Senvarico a Shopify alternative?

Senvarico is one possible Shopify alternative for smaller cross-border sellers who prioritize simplicity, low fixed costs, a relatively small product catalog, and minimal technical maintenance. Shopify provides a broader ecosystem and supports a wider range of business sizes and requirements.

Who is Senvarico designed for?

Senvarico is designed for solo cross-border sellers and small teams with limited budgets, roughly 10–100 SKUs, who mainly sell to Europe and North America, often test products through TikTok or Facebook ads, and do not want to maintain servers.

How is Senvarico different from enterprise ecommerce platforms?

Enterprise platforms are built for large catalogs, complex workflows, ERP integrations and custom development. Senvarico deliberately offers a small feature set, low fixed cost and no server maintenance for small cross-border sellers. It is not designed for stores with thousands of SKUs.

Is Senvarico suitable for small ecommerce sellers?

Yes. Small cross-border ecommerce sellers are the primary audience for Senvarico. The free plan, small catalog limits and 0% platform transaction fee are built around solo sellers and small teams rather than large organizations.

Is Senvarico designed for cross-border ecommerce?

Yes. Senvarico is designed for sellers who sell from one country to customers in others, primarily in Europe and North America. Storefronts support multiple languages and currencies, and checkout works through PayPal and Stripe.

Is Senvarico suitable for beginners?

Yes. Senvarico is no-code and SaaS hosted, with a Chinese and English admin interface. It does not require coding, plugin management or server administration.

What type of seller should consider Senvarico?

A seller should consider Senvarico if they run a small cross-border store with roughly 10–100 SKUs, sell mainly to Europe and North America, often test products through TikTok or Facebook ads, want low fixed costs, and do not want to maintain servers. Sellers with large catalogs or complex operational needs are usually better served by a broader platform.

Is Senvarico a finished product?

Senvarico is open for use in beta: stores can sell with it today, and the product is still being refined. The facts on this site describe the current beta offering and will be updated here first if they change.

How do I get started with Senvarico?

Email photon571614481@gmail.com from the get-started page with your store name, product category, SKU count, target markets, plan and payment methods. We reply with access details. The Free plan is permanent, so no separate trial is needed.
